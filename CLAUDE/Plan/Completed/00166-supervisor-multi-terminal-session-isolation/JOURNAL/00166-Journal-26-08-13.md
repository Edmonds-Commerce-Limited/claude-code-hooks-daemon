## 04:45 · finding · — — a folder-touching commit is not evidence of work

Assessed during a stale-plan sweep. My first read of this plan was WRONG and is
worth recording, because the mistake is easy to repeat.

`git log -- <plan folder>` showed a commit dated 2026-08-07, six days ago, so I
characterised 00166 as actively worked with a lagging journal — a journalling
discipline problem rather than a staleness one. It is not. That commit is
`285fc998`, Plan 00201's repo-wide identifier redaction, which touched this
folder incidentally along with much of the tree.

The last commit that was actually *about* this plan is `26e4a71f`, 2026-07-15 —
29 days ago, matching the journal gap exactly. The two signals agreed all along
and I had misread one of them.

**Takeaway**: when judging whether a plan is live, filter by commits whose
SUBJECT names the plan, not by commits that touch its folder. A repo-wide sweep
touches every folder and makes every plan look active.
