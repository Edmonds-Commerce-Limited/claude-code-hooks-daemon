## 06:56 · decision · T1.2 — follow up the own line, do not try to see the box

Reading the box back from the child's ANSI output was rejected as fragile.
Instead a submitted injection is remembered as the supervisor's own line
(text, time, follow-up count) in the machine state, recorded at decision time
in the worker so it ships by hot-reload with no host change. At the next lull
one Enter is pressed for it, two at most, fifteen seconds apart. Harmless on
an empty box; the only key that submits a stuck line. A human Enter clears
the record, as does the child going busy after a follow-up. Goal, goal-clear
and standing-auth wait while it is pending. The DROP ANCHOR emergency and the
compaction machine keep their precedence.

The goal cap became a rolling one-hour budget: the lifetime cap of five had
already been met by this session at 22:52, so the 23:08 goal was refused.
