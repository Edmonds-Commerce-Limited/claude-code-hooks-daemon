Two things worth recording because they are the durable lessons, not the diffs:

1. **The release could not pass its own gate.** Release prep dirties
   `pyproject.toml`/`version.py`/`CHANGELOG.md`, which makes the project-level
   `release_blocker` (terminal, priority 8) match and shadow `auto_continue_stop`
   (priority 10). Two acceptance probes therefore asserted a premise that was
   unreachable *precisely when they were required to run*. Fixed by isolating
   the probes' `cwd` — the tests were coupled to repo state they had no reason
   to depend on.

2. **The first fix attempt was itself the anti-pattern.** Task 4.1's unicode
   crash initially got a `try/except OSError: return None`. The `error_hiding`
   QA check rejected it, correctly: the catch was defensiveness concealing a
   real failure. `errors="replace"` fixes the actual defect and swallows
   nothing. Worth remembering that the guard fired on its own author.
