### `get_claude_md()` is ALREADY abstract

`core/handler.py` declares it `@abstractmethod`, alongside `matches`,
`handle` and `get_acceptance_tests`. The probe confirms it empirically: all
107 classes define it (`overrides=True` for every single one).

This corrects Task 3.1's stated rationale. A new handler CANNOT silently
escape *the method* — the ABC makes that impossible. What it can escape is
*triage*: `return None` satisfies the compiler in five seconds and records
nothing about whether the question was considered.

So the gate is still worth building, but its precise job is narrower and
clearer than the plan states: **turn an unexplained `return None` into an
explained one**. The 52 Nones are not 52 omissions — they are 52 decisions
with no recorded reasoning, which is why a release-time human had to
re-derive them by hand.

## Phases 2 and 3 — closing entry

### The gate caught its own author

`ValidatePlanNumberHandler` went into `_EARNS_GUIDANCE` on my inference that
the plan-workflow handlers were all covered. It returns `None`. The gate
failed the entry within a minute of being written, and reading the handler
confirmed `None` is right: `plan_number_helper`'s resident section already

Worth recording because it is the argument for the whole approach. I had
already looked at the injected marker list and seen `validate-plan-number`
absent, and still wrote the wrong verdict fifteen minutes later. A table
checked against the code catches that; a table of prose does not.

### Two things measured that the plan assumed

- **`get_claude_md()` is `@abstractmethod`.** Task 3.1's premise ("a new
  handler could silently escape triage") was half wrong: the method cannot be
  missing. Only the reasoning can be.
- **The resident block is 68% of `CLAUDE.md`.** The plan asked whether six
  sections "earn their context cost" without anyone having measured the cost.
  With the number in hand the answer flipped from "add six" to "add none of
  the six, and add two others the audit never examined".

### Discovery must not be tolerant

`measure_instruction_footprint.py` (Plan 00116) already enumerates handlers
and silently skips any it cannot construct. Correct for a measurement — a
missing row skews a trend slightly. Fatal for a guard: a handler that drops
out of discovery is exactly the silent escape the guard exists to catch. The
new suite raises instead, and its stale-entry check doubles as a vacuity
guard so an empty discovery cannot pass green.

### The auto-commit trap, live

Restarting the daemon auto-committed the regenerated `CLAUDE.md` (`a3a3e6fa`)
while the two handler sources were still uncommitted. That is precisely the
hazard RELEASING.md Step 13 warns about — generated artifact in git, source
not — and it happened within a minute of the restart. Committing the source
immediately is the only thing that keeps the two in step.
