## REFUTED / imprecise (most consequential first)

1. **Claim 7: "The count is kept per session, per class".** The counter is keyed by session and EVENT (`Stop` vs `SubagentStop`), not by reason class.
   - `_stop_counter_path(daemon_root, session, event)` is at `init.sh:642`, and it is used at `init.sh:738`.
   - `test_alternating_reasons_count_as_three` in `test_init_sh_daemon_down_stop_bound.py` shows that "not running" and "venv missing" blocks share one count.
   - The stand-in ruling says the same: "the CLASS of block, not its text" is what gets counted together.
   - Change to the plan: replace "per class" with "per session and event (Stop and SubagentStop counted separately); all daemon-unreachable reasons share the count". Otherwise a reader will expect separate counts per reason.
2. **Claim 11: "S2: SubagentStop shares one counter".** This is imprecise, though not false in substance.
   - Stop and SubagentStop do NOT share a counter (`test_stop_and_subagent_stop_do_not_share_a_count`).
   - The finding in the review is that concurrent sub-agents of one session share the single SubagentStop counter, and that the parent's `transcript_path` is read.
   - Change to the plan: reword to "concurrent sub-agents of one session share the SubagentStop counter".

Minor: the Task 2.6 list of unbounded blocks ("CI-enforced, invalid-input and needs-provision") is incomplete, not wrong. The code also keeps REPO_UNCONFIGURED and VERSION_MISMATCH out of the bound (`init.sh:1253-1256`).
