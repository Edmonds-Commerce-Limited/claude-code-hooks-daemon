The allowlist, with the reason for each entry (`shell_expansion._INERT_HEADS`):

- `cd`, `pushd`, `popd`, `pwd`: they only move or print the working directory.
- `echo`, `true`, `false`, `:`: they only print or ignore their arguments.
- `set`: allowed without `-x` or `xtrace`, because a trace expands PS4, and PS4 can hold a substitution.
- `export`: allowed with plain `NAME` or `NAME=value` operands only. `-f` exports functions, and `a[$(x)]=1` runs `$(x)`.
- `mkdir`, `ls`, `cat`, `grep`, `sleep`: none of them runs anything.
- Python: it can do nothing the exempted program cannot do already.

Deliberately left off the list: `printf` (`printf -v 'a[$(x)]'`), `test`/`[` (`-v` evaluates a subscript), `git` (aliases and hooks), and every other name.
