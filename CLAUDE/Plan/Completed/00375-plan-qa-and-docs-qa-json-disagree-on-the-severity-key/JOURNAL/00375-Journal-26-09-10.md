## 16:10 · finding · T1.1 — the two enums never disagreed on VALUES, only on the key

- `src/claude_code_hooks_daemon/daemon/cli.py:5515` — `plan-qa`, `"level"`
- `src/claude_code_hooks_daemon/daemon/cli.py:5658` — `docs-qa`, `"severity"`

Everything else in the payload is identical (`check_id`, `message`,
`remediation`, `path`), and the enums agree exactly:

```

dangerous to leave — the two payloads are otherwise indistinguishable to a
reader, so nothing else signals that a key is missing.
