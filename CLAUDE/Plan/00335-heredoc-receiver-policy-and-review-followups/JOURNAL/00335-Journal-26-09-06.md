## 21:20 · finding · P1 — a fourth executor bypass, found before deciding

Probed the shipped handler before choosing between the two Phase 1 directions,
rather than reasoning from the review alone. `probe_phase1.py`:

```
ALLOW   | ['ssh', 'host']  | ssh remote execution
ALLOW   | ['b$ash']        | expansion receiver b$'ash'
ALLOW   | ['SHELL']        | expansion receiver $SHELL
DENY    | ['jq', '.']      | jq identity filter (data)
```

`ssh host <<'EOF'` executes the body on the REMOTE host and was ALLOWED. It is
the same class as B2's `eval` / `. /dev/stdin` and was on neither list, so a
`curl … | bash` payload passed a priority-10 terminal RCE guard. Nobody found
it in three review passes — which is itself the argument: a family that has
now yielded a fourth member after three rounds of enumeration is not
enumerable.

Also checked and NOT a bypass: `cat <<'EOF' | bash` denies today (the
heredoc-body pattern does not match that shape at all, so nothing is blanked).

## 21:35 · decision · T1.1 — inversion, and it swallowed two other tasks

   separately wasteful.
