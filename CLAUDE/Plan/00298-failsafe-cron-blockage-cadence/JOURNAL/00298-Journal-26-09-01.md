## 06:30 · action · — — PLAN.md and BRAINSTORM.md filled in

Filed at the owner's direct instruction after last night's incident: the
hourly failsafe recovery cron fired ~14 times overnight while the session
was stably blocked only on two pending human decisions, each tick costing a
full model turn to produce a guaranteed no-op ("token burning for the sake
of it once we hit a full blockage").

Read `recovery_cron_advisor.py` (post_tool_use) and `auto_continue_stop.py`
(stop) to ground the brainstorm. Key finding: the waste is NOT a deny-loop —
`auto_continue_stop.py` Branch 2 cleanly allows a `STOPPING BECAUSE:`
explained stop — the waste is the per-tick model turn itself, since the
cron's canonical prompt reaches the model on every tick regardless of
session state.

Wrote `BRAINSTORM.md` evaluating 5 ideas (session-side backoff, daemon-side
`UserPromptSubmit` suppression, a Stop-recorded blocked-state marker, cadence
baked into the cron prompt, and explicit distinction of blockage types).
Recommendation: the blocked-state marker (Stop handler) + UserPromptSubmit
suppression keyed on the canonical cron prompt text is the only
deterministic, zero-token option; session/prompt-text-only backoff ideas are
convention-only and still cost a turn per tick, so they're documented as
fallback only, not primary.

Filled in `PLAN.md`: Overview cites the incident evidence, Goals/Non-Goals
scope this plan to design-only (no daemon source edits here — that's a
follow-on plan per `src/CLAUDE.md`'s daemon-source restriction), Phase 1
is owner review of the brainstorm, Phase 2 is filing the implementation
plan once confirmed.
