## Stated limit (not a defect this plan can close)

PreCompact fires when compaction STARTS. A Tab-completed `/compact` queued
behind a streaming turn is unseen until it runs. In the elevated or critical
band the supervisor may type its own `/compact` in that window. This is the
duplicate-not-miss cost PLAN.md already accepts, and it is recorded in Task 1.1,
`CLAUDE/development/CcySupervisor.md` and release note 50.
