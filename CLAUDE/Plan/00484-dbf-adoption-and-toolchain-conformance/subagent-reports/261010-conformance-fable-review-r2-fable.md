## Ruling on the 6.2b decline

**The fixer is right, and round 1 was wrong on the fact.** `config/exception_entries.py`
(`_pattern_of_mapping`) calls `is_acceptable_reason` on every `{pattern, reason}` entry and raises
`ValueError`, which is a config load error, and `docs/guides/HANDLER_REFERENCE.md:44` documents it
("A placeholder reason (`tbd`, `because`, `n/a` and similar) is a config error"). So a documented
generic-reason check does exist on config exceptions. PARTLY MET at both levels is the right grade:
the check reaches every form that carries a reason (hatches, inline directives, `{pattern, reason}`)
and cannot reach the reason-less forms (`enabled: false`, `mode: warn`, bare strings), which the row
correctly files under 6.2a; it checks for placeholders, not that a reason "names hazard and scope",
and it does not verify truth. The draft §9.2 still declares the residual ("applies only where a
reason is given"), so nothing is hidden by the regrade. The five-NOT-MET sentence and the counts
were derived with 6.2b at PARTLY and are consistent.
