## 19:40 · decision · T1.3 — "cannot fire" is too strong for yolo

The audit called it structurally unable to fire. That is accurate for a
default install, but a client who sets `show_on_session_start: true` DOES get
the banner. The honest framing is "off by default, and nothing turns it on".

Removing it anyway, because Plan 00128 already made this judgement once: it
turned the banner off on the grounds that the container is surfaced better by
the status-line icon and by `hooks-daemon check`. This completes that decision
rather than making a new one. The `RETIRED_HANDLERS` entry names the loss for
anyone who had flipped the flag.

## 20:00 · decision · P1 — an event with no handlers keeps its config section

`test_config_has_all_event_types` wanted a `session_end` section. Two ways to
satisfy it: drop the event from the expected set, or keep an empty section.

Kept the empty section (`session_end: {}`, matching what `init_config` now
generates). SessionEnd is still a registered, dispatchable event — the hook
fires and reaches the daemon — it simply has no handlers today. Dropping it
from the expected set would have weakened an invariant to make a symptom go
away, and would leave a project-level SessionEnd handler with no documented
place to live.
