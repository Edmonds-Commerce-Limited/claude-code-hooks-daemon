## Scope and method

- Source: `/root/.claude/projects/-workspace/*.jsonl`, 5 main-thread transcripts. Sub-agent transcripts in subdirectories were not read. Records with `isSidechain` were skipped.
- Span: 2026-09-15 09:28 to 2026-10-10 16:04 UTC. The three large sessions are one chain: 9679b063 (Sep 15-16), e5e72775 (Sep 16 - Oct 6), c61663f0 (Oct 6-10).
- One request is one distinct `requestId`. Streamed blocks of one request are merged. Assistant records with `isApiErrorMessage` are NOT requests: they are synthetic HTTP 429 usage-limit errors (423 records), excluded and counted separately.
- Gap is start-of-request to start-of-next-request, which is what the cache-gaps tool measures. The end-to-start gap differs by 1-2 seconds at the median and under 1% at the tails, so the choice does not matter.
- Excluded: 120a0cfb (no successful request, one 429 record). ec5d431d is included but negligible (5 requests, 4 gaps, max 10.6s).
- 24,075 gaps in total.

## Gaps coinciding with dropped ticks

What the data holds, plainly:

1. The rule IDs `R-FAILSAFE-CRON-SUPPRESSED`, `R-FAILSAFE-CRON-BACKED-OFF`, `R-DECLARED-CRON-SUPPRESSED` and `R-USAGE-PAUSE-PROMPT` appear in NO log under `/workspace/untracked/`. I grepped `logs/hooks/verdicts.jsonl`, `stop-events.jsonl` and `cron-records.json` (the daemon-log candidates; I did not recurse the whole tree because a protected-path guard blocks recursive search). The verdict log records handler names and event, and its `rule` field is null for these handlers.
2. The verdict log covers only 2026-10-09 07:54 to 2026-10-10 16:04 UTC, against transcripts from 2026-09-15. It cannot speak for e5e72775 or 9679b063.
3. Handler events in that window, joined to the request gap containing each event:
   - `usage-pause-gate`, UserPromptSubmit, verdict deny: 28 events, 2026-10-09 23:51 to 2026-10-10 08:51. All 28 lie inside ONE gap of 53,403s (2026-10-09 18:45 to 2026-10-10 09:35). That gap holds 17 synthetic 429 errors and 44 `scheduled_task_fire` records, and only 2 real assistant records. This is presumably the `R-USAGE-PAUSE-PROMPT` deny, but the log does not say so.
   - `failsafe-cron-blockage-suppressor`, UserPromptSubmit, verdict allow: 23 events (allow, not deny; whether it drops the tick by another route is not visible). Containing gaps: p50 434s, 13 over 5m, 5 over 60m (max 53,403s).
4. Independent transcript-side estimate: a `scheduled_task_fire` record whose task produced no delivered tick user record before its next fire. c61663f0: 156 fires, 125 delivered, 31 with no delivery, 28 of them inside the same 53,403s gap. e5e72775: 1,012 fires, 984 delivered, 29 with none, 28 inside gaps over 5m (median containing gap 3,592s, max 4,260s). 9679b063: 68 fires, 0 undelivered. These are fires with no matching injection. They include ticks the daemon dropped but also ticks lost to a 429 outage or coalesced, so this is an upper bound on drops, not a count of them.
5. Effect: a dropped tick is the removal of a warming event, so gaps containing dropped or undelivered ticks are the long ones. The c61663f0 outage gap is the one place where dropped prompts and a gap far beyond 60 minutes coincide.

## What this data does and does not answer for 00452 Task 2.4

Does answer:

- This always-on, cron-driven session's own gap distribution with percentiles, tail shares over both TTLs, and a per-ender breakdown.
- Confirms the circularity: tool loops, ticks and task-notifications end the large majority of gaps; the only gaps over 1h without a tick-delivery failure were compacts, human returns and long tool calls.
- Names the mechanism that produces long cold gaps here: usage-limit outages where ticks error instead of warming, plus dropped or undelivered ticks.
- Per-request chance of crossing 5m is 5.2%; crossing 1h is 0.03% outside outages.
