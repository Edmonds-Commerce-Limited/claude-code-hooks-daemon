## REFUTED (most consequential first)

### R1. "Plan 00488 added lines above the three init.sh suppressions, which moved them from 1215/2566/2676 to 1228/2653/2763" (N426 Found paragraph) as the cause of the CI failure

The CI run failed at 9de5ed59a, and at that commit the lines were 1222 / 2573 / 2683, not 1228 / 2653 / 2763.

- `git show 9de5ed59a:init.sh` gives `shellcheck disable=SC1091` at 1222 and SC2317 at 2573 and 2683.
- The test at 9de5ed59a still pinned 1215 / 2566 / 2676 (`git show 9de5ed59a:tests/unit/qa/test_check_inline_suppressions.py`, lines 62-64). That matches the failing IDs in the CI logs: `[init.sh-1215]`, `[init.sh-2566]`, `[init.sh-2676]` (ci-69-312.log:2996, 3014, 3034).
- The commit that moved them by +7 was 4b54675ed, "Status line stays visible while the daemon is down, and names the outage". It was committed 2026-10-11 19:52 and is an ancestor of 9de5ed59a. Its init.sh hunks are at line 40 (+7) and at lines 2731 and 2742, which are below the first two suppressions.
- The four Plan 00488 commits that touch init.sh (769c6fde0, 50e3adeec, 2f075662d, 1e7cd642e) were committed between 20:09 and 20:45. All four come after 9de5ed59a (20:03); `git log 9de5ed59a..HEAD -- init.sh` lists exactly these four.
- Plan 00488 therefore did not cause the CI failure. It moved the lines a second time, to 1228 / 2653 / 2763, which are the current HEAD positions (`grep -n 'shellcheck disable' init.sh` gives 1228, 2653, 2763). Commit 0a8be8afe's new numbers are correct for HEAD, but the test was already stale at 9de5ed59a.

What this changes in the plan:

- Attribute the original break to 4b54675ed (a status-line change), not to Plan 00488. The 1215 -> 1222 and 2566 -> 2573 and 2676 -> 2683 step is the one CI caught.
- Say that Plan 00488 later moved the lines again and that 0a8be8afe re-pinned them for HEAD.
- The first N426 line, "from 1215/2566/2676 to 1228/2653/2763", is correct as a before/after pair, but the "Plan 00488 added lines" attribution of the whole move is wrong. Plan 00488's share is 1222 -> 1228, 2573 -> 2653 and 2683 -> 2763.
- The phrase "The range QA for 00488 passed, because changed_tests does not map init.sh to this test" is true of the mapping (R2 in the table below) but it is not why CI went red. The commit that broke the test is 4b54675ed. Whether its own QA ran this test is not shown by anything checked here.
- "Found ... Plan 00488 ... moved" in the STATUS-INDEX Verdict ("Plan 00488 moved them and targeted QA never ran it, so CI went red") carries the same wrong attribution.
- The PLAN.md line "N426 (a test pinning init.sh lines, stopgap applied)" makes no attribution and is fine.
