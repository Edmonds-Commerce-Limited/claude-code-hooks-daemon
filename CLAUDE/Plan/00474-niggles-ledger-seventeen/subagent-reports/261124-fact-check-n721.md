# Fact check: N721 (NIGGLES.md, plan 00474)

## REFUTED / qualified (most consequential first)

1. Claim 5: "The refusal's own advice, 'deliberately delete each listed path', cannot clear a tracked file". The quote is not the verbatim refusal text, and the refusal also offers a route that works for tracked files. The rule-table line (destructive_git.py:1090) says "Commit, push or deliberately delete each listed path, then remove". The long text (destructive_git.py:392-393) says "commit or push the work on the lane's branch, move each listed path to a tracked home, or delete it deliberately". The deny note (destructive_git.py:797) says "commit or move each path, then retry". So "the stated remedy cannot work" overstates: delete cannot clear a dirty tracked path, but "commit" can. The entry's own Expected clause (restore or commit the path) partly duplicates existing advice. The delete-advice helper `_delete_first_advice` (line 666) covers only ignored entries, not dirty tracked ones. Fix: quote exactly, and narrow the claim to "the delete option cannot clear a tracked path".
