them (line 37). `worktree_file_copy.py` (lines 23-24) imports them from there too. The commit `1f0a6ef5b` moved the helpers into
that leaf module. Everything else in the "Done" bullet holds: no `_FIND_EXEC`, `_FIND_EXEC_FLAGS`, `_FIND_ACTIONS` or

what keeps the operand judged omits `-delete`, which the helper also refuses (`find_expression.py:49`) and which is pinned in
`TestWhatStillDenies`. That is incomplete, not false.

ledger now describes a name the tree does not have. Change to the plan: name `_GIT_MESSAGE_COMMAND`, or say N399 widened it.
