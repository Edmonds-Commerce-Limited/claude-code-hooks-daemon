The other users are `destructive_git`, `merge_qa_advisor` and `daemon_sync_after_merge`, none of which is a commit gate.
   - Change to the plan: say "the shared placement walker (`git_invocations`, `placement_problem`, `invocation_directory`) that `conflict_marker_commit_gate`, `destructive_git`, `merge_qa_advisor` and `daemon_sync_after_merge` use (start-directory handling extended by N402 (a))", or drop the N400 to N402 reference.

   - `daemon_sync_after_merge._is_foreign_repo` (line 259) says "Mirrors `merge_qa_report._is_foreign_repo`".
   - `merge_qa_report._is_foreign_repo` (line 282) says "Mirrors `staged_lint_gate._is_foreign_repo`". It does not point at `daemon_sync_after_merge`.
   - Change to the plan: the remedy should say the `daemon_sync_after_merge` docstring is stale and the `merge_qa_report` docstring should be updated to describe the new merge-directory resolution. It should not describe a mutual pointing.
