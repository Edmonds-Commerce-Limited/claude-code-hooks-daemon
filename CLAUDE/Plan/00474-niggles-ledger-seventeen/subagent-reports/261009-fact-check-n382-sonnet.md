It reads `CLAUDE/HANDLER_DEVELOPMENT.md` and `constants.priority` (lines 23-26, 69). Its `test_no_handler_priority_falls_outside_every_band` (line 120) is the only part that could involve handlers. I did not trace it.
  - So "walk the handler package" is exact for two of the four. Change the wording to "enumerate every handler (by package walk or `HandlerRegistry.discover`)". Drop priority bands, or confirm it by reading `test_no_handler_priority_falls_outside_every_band`.
