  `_FLAG_COMPACT_BACKOFF_SECONDS=1800`; machine `_flag_compactions` counter with
  `mark_flag_compaction` / `flag_compact_due`, exported+imported in state.

  branch (Decision 3). Gated `payload is None and NOOP and signal_path is None
  and state is MONITOR and machine.flag_compact_due(now)`. Fires
  `Decision.WOULD_COMPACT` with `/compact {bot-prefix} {_FLAG_COMPACT_BODY}`
  (armed) or the dry-run marker; arms audit `/compact (flag-cleaning after
  repeated downgrade)`; `can_inject` guard defers when the box is busy.

  (default False). The capacity `/compact` (Plan 00152) leaves it False and
  keeps its `AWAIT_COMPACTING` lifecycle; only the flip-flop compact sets it,

  Field round-trips through the worker→host JSON.

  no ESC interrupt is needed, and the resume is driven by the compaction signal
  (`reading.compacting` → `WOULD_CONTINUE`) exactly like every other `/compact`
  — verified the resume path does not require the `AWAIT_COMPACTING` state.
