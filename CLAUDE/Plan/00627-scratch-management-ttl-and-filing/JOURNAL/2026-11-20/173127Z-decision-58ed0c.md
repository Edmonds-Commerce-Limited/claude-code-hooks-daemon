## 17:31 · decision · — — Task 3.0 N4 closed: sub-root spelling

Task 3.0, N4 closed (sub-root spelling). The reference grep now spells each separator between the root's components as `/+([.]/+)*`, so `untracked/scratch/./sub`, `untracked/scratch//sub` and `untracked//scratch/sub` all match under `--root untracked/scratch/sub`. Each match is read in every spelling (stripped of trailing `._+@~/-`, tidied of empty and `.` segments, and dot-resolved), and any spelling under the root names its entry; a spelling equal to the root names every entry. A match whose every spelling lies outside the root now names nothing instead of raising UNKNOWN (the widened pattern legitimately returns siblings such as `subway`). Tests: the same five odd spellings name the entry under the sub-root and under the default root; four odd spellings of the root itself name every entry; odd spellings of a sibling name none. Residual: `untracked/scratch/../scratch/sub/x` (a `..` before the root's last component) still does not match under a sub-root; DESIGN calls only `./`, `//` and trailing dots/slashes in scope.
