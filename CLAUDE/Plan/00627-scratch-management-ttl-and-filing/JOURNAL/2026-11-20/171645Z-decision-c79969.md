F-BYPS 2, the sweep-level cross-entry pass (`core/scratch_sweep.py`).

`_judge_entry` now also returns `_Pointers`, which say where the entry's `.git` files and `commondir` files point. Each pointer is read with `read_small_regular_file`, and both its lexical target and its resolved target are mapped to an entry of the root. The pointer text is never reported. `_with_dependencies` runs after every entry is judged. A DISPOSABLE, UNCERTAIN or REFERENCED entry that another entry points into becomes PROTECTED. Its reason names the pointing file, and its proofs and filing proposal are dropped because the proposal was made on the old verdict. Some pointers cannot be known: an unreadable or non-regular pointer, or an entry not walked to the end. Then every DISPOSABLE row becomes STUCK and the report is incomplete. This fails closed, and a budget-cut walk now holds back every disposal in that run.

Tests (`TestCrossEntryDependencies`): standalone `a/repo` with `b/.git` pointing into it (a was DISPOSABLE at RED); a relative pointer; an in-entry linked worktree that changes nothing; a FIFO `.git` that makes `a` STUCK; and a refused directory elsewhere that makes a P1 entry STUCK. `test_cli_scratch_sweep`'s read-only test now accepts STUCK for `realclone` only with the cross-entry reason.

Residual: `objects/info/alternates` that borrow from another entry are not mapped (recorded in DESIGN 13).
