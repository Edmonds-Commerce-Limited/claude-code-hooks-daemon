> findings, decisions, dead-ends, hand-off state — the linear lifecycle a plan

> **Entry grammar** — append new entries at the BOTTOM; NEVER edit earlier
> entries (corrections are new entries):

> ```

> - `category` ∈ `action` | `finding` | `decision` | `thought` | `blocker` | `handoff`

> - Bodies may embed fenced logs/diffs/snippets — no size limit — with a
>   one-line takeaway above the fence.
> - End a working session with a `handoff` entry so the next agent's entry
>   point is the last entry of the newest day-file.

## 13:20 · decision · — — the ledger exists because chat is not a record

Owner's ruling: "record ALL defects, never just casually tell me about them
without recording them… we should always have an active plan that is
collecting small niggles… NEVER just report niggles in context output, they
MUST be recorded in the niggles plan."

The failure being closed is specific and was happening repeatedly in this
session: a defect noticed in passing got a sentence in chat and nothing else.
That reads as diligence while producing the same result as silence — the
context window rolls and the finding is gone.

SOP written into the DAEMON-OWNED baseline
(`install/templates/core/PlanWorkflow.core.md`, "The niggles ledger") rather
than this repo's own layer, so every client project inherits it. Core
Principle 9 added: "Record Every Defect".

kept and its rationale left intact — it is correct for the DUPLICATE question

a skipped check.
