# Plan 00537 fact-check 2 (diff claims vs .claude/ccy/claude-supervise.py)

## REFUTED

R1. "the usage-pause send ... keeps its bypass of the idle gate" (and the Out-of-scope line "usage-pause bypass of the idle gate"). Refuted as worded.
`_usage_pause_outcome` (lines 6739-6752) requires `facts.idle and facts.work_idle` and `facts.input_line_empty` before it produces a payload. That is the same pair of conditions as `can_inject` (line 6854). It bypasses the machine's `evaluate` and the injection families (line 6864), not the idle gate. It is the one send that never passes through `can_inject`, and it does re-check idle on its own. Plan change: say it "bypasses `can_inject` and the families, but applies its own idle + work_idle + empty-box gate", so the focus gate must be added in `_usage_pause_outcome` itself.
