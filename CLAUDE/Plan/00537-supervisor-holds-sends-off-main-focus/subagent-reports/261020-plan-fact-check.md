## REFUTED / needs correction (most consequential first)

1. **"Every send path that bypasses `can_inject`" lists the ESC payload and the resubmit payload as bypasses. They do not bypass it.**
   `can_inject = facts.idle and facts.input_line_empty` (line 6854) is passed to `machine.evaluate(idle=can_inject)` (6887). In `_evaluate_await`, both WOULD_ESCAPE and WOULD_RESUBMIT are produced only under `if idle and self._escape_due(now)` (6119-6150). So ANDing the latch into `can_inject` already gates them. The real bypasses are:

   - the abandoned-box flush (6054-6076): it fires when `idle` is False;
   - the model switch (gate is `facts.input_line_empty` only, 7125-7145);
   - the restart `/exit` (8692), which never sees `can_inject`.
     Change: reword the Goals bullet and the Task 1.3 families. Keep a test that ESC/resubmit defer, but call it covered by the `can_inject` AND, not a separate gate.

2. **Success criterion "No `_perform_injection` call site sends while the latch is set" versus the Non-Goal "weakening ... the usage-pause bypass".**
   `decide_once` returns `_usage_pause_outcome` (6862-6864) before the families run, and the resulting payload is sent through `_apply_decision` at 7780. A latch ANDed into `can_inject` therefore does not hold that /compact. The plan must say whether the host-side gate lives in `_apply_decision`/`_perform_injection`'s caller (which would hold the usage-pause send too, contradicting the non-goal) or whether the criterion carves out the usage-pause send.

3. **Minor: "the abandoned-box Enter flush, gated before its red-or-worse check."**
   In `evaluate` the order is: reading None, stale, not red (6043-6048), then `foreground_ambiguous` (6052), then `not idle` plus abandoned (6054-6076). The red check already precedes the flush, so "before the red check" cannot be met by inserting a gate at the flush site. Reword to "gated at the flush, ahead of the one-shot `_abandoned_box_handled = True` latch (6069)". Gating after the latch is set would burn the one-shot while held.
