## minor 1: a brace group split across two literals is no longer enumerated

**Citation.** `shell_expansion.py:561-592` (`python_string_literals`) and `secret_file_matching.py:1514-1522`. Each literal is enumerated alone. An operator between two literals (`+`, a list passed to `''.join`), or an f-string that writes `{"{"}`, puts the `{` and the `}` of one shell brace group into different literals. No single source then holds the whole group.

**What it allows.** I built the braces at runtime and ran them through `HandlerChain` with the test module's own handler and fixture path. Main denies each of these and the branch allows them:

- `os.system('cat /proj/.vault-p{a,' + 'x}ss')`, with or without spaces around `+`, as a heredoc or as `-c`;
- the same through `operator.attrgetter`;
- the same through `pwrite` followed by `bash g.sh`;
- `''.join([...])`;
- an f-string that spells the braces as `{"{"}` and `{"}"}`.

**Why it is minor and not MAJOR.** Main caught these only by accident. Its raw-text brace scan happened to rejoin the two literals. On main, the same assembly without braces is allowed:

- `'cat /proj/.vault-pa' + 'ss'` is allowed;
- `'cat /proj/.vault-p' + '{a,x}ss'` is allowed on both trees.

So this sits inside the accepted runtime-assembly residual. The branch loses coverage that main had only by accident, and it opens no route that main blocked on purpose.

**Class.** A path spread over more than one literal and rejoined at runtime, where the old whole-text scan happened to put it back together.

**Why the tests miss it.** Every row in `TestRoundTwoFindingsAreClosed` spells the whole brace group inside one literal. Implicit concatenation (`'a{b,' 'c}d'`) is caught, because the `ast` constant holds the joined value, and that is the only multi-literal shape the tests cover.

**Detector hypothesis.** Also enumerate what you get by constant-folding each `BinOp(Add)` chain of string constants and each `str.join` over a list of constants. It will always lag new assembly routes, as the residual already does. Or record this as part of the accepted residual in NIGGLES N101. That costs nothing and is the honest option.

**Confidence.** High that the verdicts differ (probed on both trees). Medium on the grading, which depends on how the residual is defined.
