# Fact-check: commit 8cf9a051f (#106 folded into Plans 00501 and 00497)

Scope: claims added by the commit in 00501 PLAN.md, MANUAL-GOALS.md (whole file), 00497 PLAN.md. Current tree only.

## REFUTED (most consequential first)

1. **"Phase 0 gains a spike: does `UserPromptSubmit` fire for `/goal clear`?"** (MANUAL-GOALS.md, "How it fits").
   00501 PLAN.md Phase 0 (Tasks 0.1 to 0.3, PLAN.md lines 104-110) was not touched. The spike was added as Task 8.1 inside Phase 8. The
   two documents disagree on where the spike lives. Change: say "Phase 8 opens with a spike (Task 8.1)", or move the task into Phase 0.
2. **Owner question 2, "does `manual-goals` also stop the supervisor re-injecting the goal after a compaction?"** presupposes that the
   supervisor re-injects a goal after compaction. In the supervisor source (`.claude/ccy/claude-supervise.py`) the only post-compaction
   injection is `continue` (`_CONTINUE_BODY`, line ~6283). `/goal` is injected only from a `*.goal-intent` signal (lines 23-25, 1198,
   6974 "Strictly SUBORDINATE to compact/continue"). Nothing links `/goal` to a compaction. Plan 00474's own re-check
   (`CLAUDE/Plan/00474-niggles-ledger-seventeen/subagent-reports/261011-n334-recheck-sonnet.md`, lines 8-9) says the same. The daemon's
   `goal_injection.py` re-asserts a goal on a plan touch or a same-session resume. Whether a goal survives an in-session compaction is
   undetermined without a live compaction (same report, line 32). Change: reword question 2 as "the daemon's resume or plan-touch
   re-assertion and the signal-driven supervisor `/goal`", and drop "supervisor re-injecting after compaction" unless the owner's
   comment says otherwise (see U-row 3).
3. **Precedence (partial).** MANUAL-GOALS.md says `manual-goals` "sits in the precedence list between `awaiting-human` and `focus`".
   The list exists (00501 PLAN.md line 71: `usage-paused > goal-blocked > awaiting-human > focus > unattended`) and the two named neighbours are
   adjacent, so the placement is coherent. But the commit did not add `manual-goals` to that list, the "The modes:" line (line 22) or the
   suppression matrix. The status-line text stays stale until a task updates it. Change: note that Task 8.3 must amend line 71 and the
   design report's matrix.
