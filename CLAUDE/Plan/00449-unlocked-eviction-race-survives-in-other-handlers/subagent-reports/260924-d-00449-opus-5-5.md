## Design notes

- `BoundedFifoMap` (`src/claude_code_hooks_daemon/handlers/utils/bounded_fifo_map.py`)
  is a `MutableMapping` with every operation under an `RLock`, and iteration
  over a snapshot. Every insert path evicts the oldest entry in the same
  critical section. `put(..., journal=)` and `insert_if_absent(..., journal=)`
  snapshot the victim BEFORE the new key, so a rollback exactly restores a full
  map. Tests: `TestJournalledInsert`, plus a denied-call test at each
  journalled site. The `RLock` is re-entrant because `journal.snapshot`
  reads the map back while the lock is held.
- `get_or_insert` is the atomic `setdefault`. Overriding `setdefault` itself
  failed pyright's `reportIncompatibleMethodOverride`. The inherited
  `setdefault` still bounds and cannot raise.
- `git_context_injector` re-inserts on every injection, so FIFO order is
  "least recently injected", the policy its `min()` scan had. Its test fails
  on a naive in-place overwrite.
- `tests/thread_contention.py` is the shared switch-interval driver.
  `test_session_advice_counter.py` still carries its own copy of the fixture.
  It passes, and I left it alone.
