# Callout: a team coordinator's waiting stop must first tell the owner on the bus

**Plan**: 00636
**Audience**: operators

New Stop handler `owner_wait_stop_guard` (priority 3, enabled by default). In a teamchat team where the role probe says this session is the `coordinator`, a stop that waits on a human (a `STOPPING BECAUSE:` line waiting on someone in ordinary wording, such as "blocked on the owner" or "need the user to pick", or the awaiting-human mode; the target must be an owner, human, user or operator, an `@handle`, or a name listed in `options.owner_names`, so "waiting for Renovate" or "pending CI response" is not a wait) is refused until every open owner-facing thread the seat asked or owns got a notifying post from it, with a real body, within `options.window_minutes` (default 30). A body of only mentions, thread ids and bump words does not count. The refusal names the threads, the one `teamchat bump --team <team> ...` command and the `pingbus thread ... --ask` forms, then says to stop again. Only a stop re-entering after this guard's own refusal is passed. Declared workers, sub-agents and sessions in no team are untouched; an unknown role, an unresolved probe or an unreadable stop message keeps the guard armed, and a fault while checking a coordinator's stop refuses it once.

**On pingbus v1 the default allows with an advisory.** `options.on_unreadable_bus` defaults to `auto`: a wait the bus cannot confirm is refused once where the adapter can read the notify flag, and otherwise allowed with an advisory naming the unchecked threads. The v1 adapter cannot read the flag, so every waiting coordinator stop gets the advisory, which costs one extra continue turn. Set `refuse` to refuse every unconfirmed wait once (on v1, every waiting coordinator stop), or `allow` to fail open with only a WARNING.
